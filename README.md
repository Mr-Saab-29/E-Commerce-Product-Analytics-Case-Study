# Product Analytics & Customer Insights Case Study

## Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Product Analytics case study conducted on an e-commerce dataset as part of a Product Data Scientist interview process.

The objective was to analyze user behavior, revenue trends, customer journeys, device usage patterns, customer lifetime value (CLV), and segmentation opportunities to generate actionable business recommendations for growth.

The analysis combines exploratory data analysis, customer analytics, funnel analysis, segmentation, and business-oriented decision making.

---

## Business Questions

The study focuses on answering the following questions:

1. How is the product catalog positioned based on price distribution?
2. How has revenue evolved over time and what factors influence its fluctuations?
3. How have customer device preferences changed throughout the year?
4. How does the mobile application perform compared to mobile web browsing?
5. How can Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) be estimated and leveraged?
6. Which customer segments should be targeted to improve growth and retention?

---

## Analytical Areas

### Product Price Distribution Analysis

- Analyzed product price distributions across all purchases.
- Identified the majority of products within lower price ranges.
- Inferred likely business categories based on pricing patterns.
- Evaluated product positioning and potential customer purchasing behavior.

### Revenue Analysis

Investigated:

- Revenue trends over time
- Peak and low-performing periods
- Seasonal purchasing behavior
- Regional revenue differences
- Product-category contribution to revenue

Key analyses included:

- Revenue by week
- Revenue by day and hour
- Revenue by region
- Revenue by product category

---

### Customer Segmentation

Performed customer segmentation using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis.

Customer groups:

- Best Customers
- General Customers
- At-Risk Customers

Additional segmentation based on customer value:

- High Value Customers
- Mid-Tier Customers
- Low Value Customers

Objectives:

- Identify churn risks
- Prioritize retention efforts
- Improve customer targeting
- Increase customer lifetime value

---

### Customer Journey & Funnel Analysis

Analyzed user progression through the purchase funnel.

Web Funnel:

Listing → Product → Basket → Purchase

Mobile App Funnel:

Product → Purchase

Measured:

- Drop-off rates
- Conversion rates
- Friction points
- User behavior differences between channels

Identified major abandonment stages and proposed optimization opportunities.

---

### Device Usage Analysis

Studied how customer behavior evolved across devices.

Channels analyzed:

- Desktop
- Mobile Web
- Android Application
- Other Devices

Investigated:

- App adoption trends
- Desktop usage patterns
- Device migration behavior
- Tracking inconsistencies following application launch

---

### Mobile App vs Web Performance

Compared application and mobile web performance using:

- Unique users
- Revenue generated
- Conversion rates

Objectives:

- Evaluate impact of app launch
- Understand user engagement differences
- Identify opportunities for app growth

---

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Developed a CLV estimation framework using:

- Average Purchase Value
- Purchase Frequency
- Customer Lifespan

Additional analysis:

- Repeat purchase probability estimation
- One-time buyer conversion analysis
- CLV distribution analysis

Business applications:

- Customer retention
- Loyalty programs
- Re-engagement campaigns
- VIP customer strategies

## Business Recommendations

Key recommendations generated from the analysis include:

### Growth

- Increase app adoption through targeted migration strategies
- Launch iOS applications to expand mobile reach
- Improve acquisition during peak engagement periods

### Revenue Optimization

- Implement dynamic pricing strategies
- Focus promotional campaigns during high-performing periods
- Optimize category-level pricing and bundling strategies

### Retention

- Develop loyalty and rewards programs
- Re-engage dormant users
- Create personalized offers based on customer segments

### Product Improvements

- Improve funnel conversion rates
- Reduce checkout friction
- Enhance tracking and analytics instrumentation
- Optimize customer onboarding experiences
